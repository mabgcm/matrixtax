## Books you can rely on

Stay organized and compliant with accurate bookkeeping and accounting, customized to how your business actually runs. Clean, up-to-date books make tax time faster, give you a clear picture of your cash flow, and put you in a strong position if the CRA ever asks questions.

## Bookkeeping

- Recording and categorizing income and expenses
- Bank and credit card reconciliations
- Tracking what customers owe you and what you owe suppliers
- Monthly or quarterly reports you can actually read

## Payroll

Our payroll service keeps your employees paid on time and your remittances in order.

- Regular pay runs and pay stubs
- Source deductions: CPP, EI and income tax
- Remittances to the CRA on your schedule
- T4 and T4A slips and summaries, due by the last day of February
- Records of Employment (ROEs) and WSIB reporting

## GST/HST

- Registration when your business needs it — generally once taxable revenues exceed $30,000 over four consecutive calendar quarters
- Monthly, quarterly or annual GST/HST returns
- Claiming every input tax credit you're entitled to

## Year-end financial statements

At year-end we close your books and prepare financial statements — the income statement, balance sheet and supporting schedules — that feed directly into your tax return and are ready for your bank or investors.
