## Tax filing without the guesswork

Maximize your returns and minimize your stress. We prepare and file tax returns for individuals, families, self-employed professionals and incorporated businesses — and we look ahead, so next year's return is better planned than this one.

## Personal tax returns (T1)

- Employment, pension and investment income
- Self-employment, commission and rental income
- RRSP, FHSA and other deductions
- Medical, tuition, childcare and donation credits
- Prior-year returns you still need to file

Filing on time also keeps benefits such as the Canada Child Benefit and the GST/HST credit flowing. Most individuals must file by **April 30**. If you or your spouse or common-law partner are self-employed, the filing deadline is **June 15** — but any balance owing is still due April 30.

## Corporate tax returns (T2)

- T2 corporate income tax returns and supporting schedules
- Year-end financial statements
- Owner compensation planning: salary, dividends or a mix
- Instalment planning, so there are no surprises

A corporation's T2 return is due six months after its fiscal year-end, while any balance owing is generally due two or three months after year-end. We keep track of these dates for you.

## Year-round tax planning

Good tax planning happens all year, not just in April. We review your situation and look for opportunities — from retirement and FHSA contributions to pension income splitting and the timing of business expenses — that can legally reduce your tax bill.

## What to bring

Your tax slips (T4, T5, T3 and others), receipts for deductions and credits, and last year's notice of assessment. We'll send you a personalized checklist so nothing gets missed.
