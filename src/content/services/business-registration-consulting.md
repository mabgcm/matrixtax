## Start on the right foot

Starting a new business? We guide you through registration, compliance and strategic planning, so the decisions you make on day one keep working for you as you grow.

## Choosing the right structure

Operating as a sole proprietor, a partnership or a corporation changes how you're taxed, how exposed you are to liability, and how much administration you take on. We walk you through the trade-offs using your own numbers, and help you decide when incorporating makes sense.

## Registration and setup

- Business name registration
- Incorporation — federally through Corporations Canada or provincially in Ontario
- A CRA business number and the program accounts you need: GST/HST, payroll and corporate income tax
- WSIB registration, where required

## Staying compliant

Once you're up and running we help you keep on top of recurring obligations, such as corporate annual returns, payroll and sales tax filings, and the records the CRA expects you to keep.

## Strategy and forecasting

- Startup consulting and business planning
- Budgets and cash-flow forecasts
- Financial projections to support financing applications

From business registration to strategic planning, our advice helps you grow and manage your business with confidence.
