## Don't face the CRA alone

A letter from the Canada Revenue Agency can be stressful — and ignoring it almost always makes things worse. If you're facing tax issues or a dispute with the CRA, our experienced team will represent you and work toward a fair resolution, so you can move forward with peace of mind.

## How we help

### CRA correspondence, reviews and audits

We review what the CRA is asking for, organize your documentation and respond on your behalf as your authorized representative. Clear, well-supported answers resolve most reviews quickly.

### Disagreeing with an assessment

If you believe an assessment is wrong, you can file a formal objection. Strict deadlines apply, so contact us as soon as you receive a notice of assessment you disagree with.

### Tax debt

We help you understand what you owe, explore payment arrangements with the CRA, and — where the circumstances qualify — request relief from penalties and interest.

### Voluntary disclosures

If past returns contained errors or omissions, the CRA's Voluntary Disclosures Program lets you correct them proactively and may provide relief from penalties and part of the interest. We prepare and file the disclosure for you.

### Late and unfiled returns

Behind on your filings? We'll bring your returns up to date, prioritizing the years that matter most, and help you get back in good standing.

## Act early

The sooner you get help, the more options you have. Contact us for a confidential consultation.
